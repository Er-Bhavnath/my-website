History

The Berlin Wall

Image from www.visitberlin.de

Description: The Berlin Wall Memorial is an original portion of the Berlin Wall, which divided East and West Berlin. There is an exhibition of the history of the wall and how it affected Berliners. The Wall Symbolises the Cold War, the German divvy and freedom Symbolism: Berlin Wall symbolises the Cold War, Germany’s secession. The memorial is also a site where the country was split and later reunited.

Brandenburg Gate

Image from www.istockphoto.com

Description: Brandenburg Gate was one of the most famous monuments in Berlin, and the 18th century designed it as a monument to peace. It was a Cold War flashpoint at the border between East and West Berlin. It became an emblem of German unification following the 1989 fall of the Berlin Wall. Historical Importance: The gate was occupied by historical events such as the years of the Berlin Wall and Germany’s unification.

Checkpoint Charlie

Image from www.orte-der-einheit.de

Description: Checkpoint Charlie was one of the most famous Cold War borders between East and West Berlin. It was a monument to the partition of the city and the confrontation between Soviet and US forces. Historical Value: The checkpoint was a site of political drama, most prominently in the Berlin Crisis of 1961, and is a popular tourist site with a museum devoted to the Berlin Wall and escape attempts.

. Holocaust Memorial

Image from www.theolinstudio.com

Description: The moving monument is made of 2,711 concrete slabs of different heights representing the colossal death toll of the Holocaust. It’s close to the Brandenburg Gate and the Reichstag. Historic Importance: The Holocaust Memorial is a solemn monument to Nazi horrors and memorial to the Holocaust.

parliament building of Germany

Image from en.wikipedia.org

Description: Reichstag (late 19th century) is the parliament building of Germany (Bundestag). It was badly destroyed in the fire of 1933 and subsequently rebuilt. Today, it has a glass dome with views of Berlin. Historical Notes: The Reichstag sits at the heart of German politics and is a site of the rise of the Nazi party and the German unification.

Events

Festival of Lights

Image from festival-of-lights.d

When: October What Tourists See: This is one of the most popular festivals in Berlin where all the landmarks in Berlin like Brandenburg Gate, Berlin Cathedral and TV Tower are bathed in beautiful light effects and projections. Tourist Experience: Wander around the city during the night, witness the marvellous light shows and get the distinct atmosphere of Berlin’s old quarter.

Berlin International Film Festival

Image from https://www.gettyimages.de/

When: February Tourists Come To Berlin: As one of the most renowned film festivals in the world, Berlinale annually brings the biggest names in film, celebrities and film fans to Berlin. It shows international premieres of the best of international film, and audiences can attend screenings, red-carpet celebrations and discussions. Tourist Attraction: Watch top movies, meet the professionals, glamour parties and screenings.

Berlin Marathon

Image from www.scc-berlin.de

When: Late September Why Tourists Go: It is one of the most famous marathons in the world with tens of thousands of runners coming from all over the world. The race is famous for its flat, fast course and is an ideal event for elites as well as amateurs. Tourist Activity: Take part in the race, watch from the sideline or feel the excitement of the event among thousands of crowd. The marathon itself is very party-like with musical entertainment and festivities.

Carnival of Cultures

Image from www.visitberlin.de

When: June Reasons Tourists Go: A multicultural street festival in Berlin celebrating Berlin’s variety. There is a massive procession of costumes, songs and performances from all across the world. Tourist Tour: Visit the parade, have International food, visit various music and shows all over the weekend.

Christmas Markets

Image from www.reiseland-niedersachsen.de

When: Late November to December The Tourist’s Reason For Coming: Berlin’s Christmas markets are famous all over the world, and you will find magical decorations, crafts, food, and more at the markets in Berlin. The markets that sell most are Gendarmenmarkt and Charlottenburg Palace. Tourist Attraction: Come for the warm winter atmosphere, souvenir shopping, and taste winter eats such as mulled wine (Glühwein), roast chestnuts, Christmas cookies.
